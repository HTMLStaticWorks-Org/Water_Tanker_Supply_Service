Transparency Policy

Additional Charges & Delivery Surcharges

We believe in 100% upfront pricing. Here is how distance, high-lift pumping, and emergency responses are billed:

Distance Fee Beyond 5 KM

$2.50 / additional km

First 5 km from the nearest regional dispatch depot is included in standard base pricing. Beyond 5 km, fuel surcharge is calculated at $2.50 per kilometre.

Express Emergency Dispatch (Under 60 Min)

+$20.00 flat fee

Applies when immediate priority dispatch is requested ahead of scheduled queue during peak hours or middle-of-the-night emergency responses.

High-Lift Vertical Pumping (4th Floor & Above)

+$15.00 flat fee

Basement sump filling and ground level tanks have zero extra charge. Pumping vertically above 4 floors requires auxiliary pressure booster setup.

Note on Seasonal Adjustments: Final rates depend on exact delivery location and daily reservoir levels. Rates confirmed via our instant quote tool are strictly locked.
